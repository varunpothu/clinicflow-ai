
# Personas

## Patient

Needs a simple way to describe when they want an appointment and receive an understandable confirmation.

Permissions: create and view their own administrative appointment requests and appointments. No access to staff or audit data.

## Receptionist

Needs an approval queue, availability visibility, the ability to modify proposals and the ability to resolve operational exceptions.

Permissions: manage patient scheduling workflows within their clinic scope.

## Clinician

Needs visibility of their schedule, leave and appointment details required for delivery of care.

Permissions: view their schedule and manage permitted calendar information. They do not automatically gain administrative configuration rights.

## Clinic Administrator

Needs to configure clinicians, appointment types, availability policies, workflow rules, users, notification templates and integration settings.

## Platform Operator

Needs telemetry, failed-job visibility, replay controls and incident runbooks without requiring routine access to patient-facing data.

## System

Represents automated services. The system role can execute only narrowly defined technical actions and cannot bypass human approval for controlled bookings.
